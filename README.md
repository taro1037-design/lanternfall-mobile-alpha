# Lanternfall Heroes & Haven — Mobile Web Alpha

This is a **playable browser test build, not a native Android APK**.

It uses the original deterministic game-core modules from the separate, private Lanternfall repository:
- Three buildings: Mine, Forge and Tavern.
- Real minute-based local earning, limited to eight hours offline.
- Real cost formulas for facility upgrades and collecting coins.
- Three starter heroes, ten stage definitions, and deterministic battle simulation.
- Local-only progress in your browser and exportable JSON.

**Limitations:** Not a Unity build. The assets/UI are placeholders for engineering playtests; original 3D art and Android optimization are pending. Browser-only coins and rewards have no monetary value and are not verified by a server. They may be manipulated and are not transferable. No account, purchases, ads, analytics, personal-data collection or paid APIs.

The compiled frontend here contains no secrets or original business plans. Do not use this web alpha as evidence that the native APK has passed Unity or Android QA.
